Why is the battery missing?

Browsers only share the battery on secure pages. In Fully Kiosk Browser, turn on its JavaScript interface (Web Content Settings). In Chrome, open chrome://flags, search Insecure origins treated as secure, add http://phonedesk.local, set it to Enabled and relaunch Chrome. A cable (USB debugging) also works.

Connect your phone

  1. On your phone, open phonedesk.local in the browser, or scan the code.
  2. Or plug it in by USB with USB debugging turned on. PhoneDesk opens on it by itself.

    Where do I find the address?

      USB and cable-free Optional

      USB debuggingOpens PhoneDesk on a plugged-in phone by itself, and links it over Wi-Fi later
      How to set it up
      1. Phone: Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times.
      2. Developer options → turn on USB debugging, plug in, accept the prompt.
      3. No cable later? Turn on Wireless debugging too. PhoneDesk links over Wi-Fi by itself.
