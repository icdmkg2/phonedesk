Why is the battery missing?

Browsers only share the battery on secure pages. In Fully Kiosk Browser, turn on its JavaScript interface (Web Content Settings). In Chrome, open chrome://flags, search Insecure origins treated as secure, add http://phonedesk.local, set it to Enabled and relaunch Chrome. A cable (USB debugging) also works.

Connect your phone

  1. On your phone, open phonedesk.local in the browser, or scan the code.
  2. Or plug it in by USB with USB debugging turned on. PhoneDesk opens on it by itself.

    Calendar

    Off

    Shows what's next beside the clock. Paste your calendar's private iCal address; it stays on this PC.

    Where do I find the address?
    1. Google Calendar: Settings → click your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format.
    2. Outlook.com / Microsoft 365: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → choose the calendar and "Can view all details" → copy the ICS link.
    3. Apple iCloud: share the calendar as a Public Calendar and copy its link.

      USB and cable-free

      Optional
      How to set it up
      1. Phone: Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times.
      2. Developer options → turn on USB debugging, plug in, accept the prompt.
      3. No cable later? Turn on Wireless debugging too. PhoneDesk links over Wi-Fi by itself.

      Spotify

      Not connected

      Up next and a ♥ button on the phone.

      How to connect
      1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard → Create app.
      2. Add Redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:8765/spotify/callback and tick Web API.
      3. Paste the Client ID below.

      Spicy Lyrics

      Off

      Word-by-word synced lyrics from the community, Apple Music and Spotify. LRCLIB stays as the fallback.

      Where do I get a key?
      1. Open developers.spicylyrics.org and sign in.
      2. Create an application. Copy its secret key (shown once).
      3. Paste it above. It stays on this PC; phones never see it.
