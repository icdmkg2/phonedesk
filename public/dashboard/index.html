    Making an old phone feel fast

    • Use USB when you can. It's faster than Wi-Fi and keeps the screen awake by itself.
    • Tap the ⤢ corner button on the phone for full screen, or use Add to Home screen in Chrome.
    • Keep the screen on over Wi-Fi: Developer options → Stay awake (while charging).
    • Close other apps on the phone and turn off battery saver while it's docked.
    • If the phone gets warm, set Dim after in Settings, or use the Black theme.
